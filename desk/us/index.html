US script

Roofers in Washington and Oregon who have no website. You're calling cold, so the first ten seconds decide it. Be short, friendly and specific to them. Calls are 9 hours behind you: their 8am is your 5pm.

Opening (they have a site built)

Hi, is this [owner first name]? It's [your name] with Digital Switch. Quick one: I build websites for roofers, and I already built one for [Business]. Can I text you the link so you can take a look?

Opening (no site built yet)

Hi, is this [owner first name]? It's [your name] with Digital Switch. I build websites for roofers. I noticed [Business] doesn't have one yet. If I built you a free preview, would you take a look? What's the best email to send it to?

Put their email in Email they gave. That's how a preview gets built and sent.

New businesses (licensed this year)

Congrats on getting licensed this year. Most new roofing companies lose jobs because customers Google them and find nothing. I'd like to fix that for you.

Find out what they think

How are you getting jobs right now? What would a website need to do for it to be worth it to you?

Listen, and log their answer in their words. This is the data that tells us how to pitch.

The offer, only after they're interested

It's $499 to set up: your own web address, set up on Google, and live within 24 hours. Then $49 a month for hosting, changes and support. No contract, cancel any time. And you see the site before you pay anything.

Close

Want me to text you the link now so you can look tonight, and we get it live this week?

Only text people who said yes on the call. Use Copy text for after they say yes on their card.

Voicemail (keep it under 20 seconds)

Hi [name], it's [your name] with Digital Switch. I built a website for [Business] and I'd love you to see it. Call or text me back at [your US number]. Thanks!

If they say…

"How much?" (early)

"$499 to set up and $49 a month, and you see it before you pay. Can I send it so you can judge for yourself?" Tag Asked the price.

"I get my work from word of mouth."

"Perfect, so do most roofers. When someone gets your name, they Google you before they call. This makes sure they find you and trust you." Tag Word of mouth.

"I'm too busy right now."

"Totally get it. When's a better time, tomorrow morning or afternoon?" Set a call-back. Tag Busy.

"Where are you calling from?"

"I run Digital Switch, a small web studio. I work remotely with roofers across Washington and Oregon. Here's my work: digitalswitch.co.za." Tag Trust.

"Take me off your list."

"Of course, sorry to bother you." Log Don't contact straight away. Never call or text that number again.
