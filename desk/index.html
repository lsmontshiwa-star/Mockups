Today's calls

Every roofer below already has a website built for them. Step 1: send 1 · First message on WhatsApp. Step 2: when they say yes, send 2 · Send the link. Step 3: once they've opened it, call them to walk them through it and close. No reply means no: don't message or call again. Anyone who replies STOP gets "Don't contact". Status saves as you tap.

Keep it one-to-one and personal. If someone says no or asks you not to contact them, tap "Don't contact" and leave it there. Landline numbers (starting 010 to 012) can't get WhatsApp, so call those and offer to send the link to a cellphone.

The script

Short, friendly, and about them. The site does the selling. Your job is to get them to look at it.

Call only people who said yes on WhatsApp

Hi, it's [your name] from Digital Switch. I sent you the website I made for [Business]. Did you get a chance to look at it?

1 · If they haven't seen it yet

Hi, is this [Business]? I'm [your name] from Digital Switch, a web design studio in Joburg. I made a free website for [Business]. May I send you the link so that you can see?

2 · Send the link while they're on the line

Great, I'm sending it now. [Tap "2 · Send the link"] Just open it on your phone, have a scroll and tell me what you think.

If they're busy: "No problem, I'll send it now and call you back at [time]. Which is better, this afternoon or tomorrow morning?"

3 · The offer (only after they've looked)

It's R2,999 to set up: we put it on your own web address, set you up on Google, and it's live within 24 hours. Then it's R199 a month for hosting, your address, changes and support. No contract, cancel any time.

4 · Close

Shall I send you the payment link so we can have it live by tomorrow?

If yes: send /pay, then /details to collect their logo, photos and service areas. Label the chat Paid once the money is in.

If they say…

"It's too expensive."

"I hear you. Most web designers charge R8,000 to R15,000 for a custom site. This is R2,999 to set up and R199 a month for hosting and updates, and it's already built for you. One extra roofing job pays for it many times over."

"I get my work from word of mouth."

"That's the best kind of work. When someone recommends you, the first thing they do is Google you. This makes sure they find you, see your work and can call you in one tap, instead of finding the next roofer."

"I have a Facebook page."

"Keep it! This works with it. A website on your own address shows up on Google, looks established, and you own it. We can link your Facebook page from it."

"Send me info, I'll think about it."

"Of course, it's on your WhatsApp now. What would you want to see changed for it to be a yes? I can tweak it today." Then follow up tomorrow with the follow-up message.

"Is this a scam? Who are you?"

"Fair question. We're Digital Switch, a web design studio in Johannesburg. You don't pay anything until you've seen your site and are happy with it. Our work is at digitalswitch.co.za, and I'm on WhatsApp at 076 129 3926."

"Not interested."

"No problem at all, thanks for your time. Have a good day." Tap "Not now" and move on. No pushing.
