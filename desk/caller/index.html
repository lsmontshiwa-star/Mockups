How it works, and what to say

Your flow: 1) Call from To call (no answer? the card has a WhatsApp ask instead). 2) Asked on WhatsApp and no reply in a day? It comes back to To call. 3) A yes, on WhatsApp or the phone, means you send their site straight away from Messages. 4) Follow up on day 2, 5 and 9 (the desk reminds you). 5) Close it from Deals, then tap Ready to pay. Lethabo sends the invoice and the site goes live.

Every WhatsApp opens on your phone with the message already written. Check it, press send, then tap I sent it here.

1. Hi, is this [business name]?

Wait for them to answer. Wrong business? Say sorry, tap Wrong number.

2. My name is [your name], I'm calling from Digital Switch. We design websites for businesses, and we found you on Google.

Pause. Let them answer.

3. We've built a preview of a website for [business name]. Can I send it to you on WhatsApp so you can have a look?

If yes: "Is this the best WhatsApp number?" Tap Said yes, then send the site from Messages right after the call.

"How much is it?" "Have a look first. If you like it, I'll send you a quote. There's no obligation."
"Who are you?" "Digital Switch. We design websites. You can see our work at digitalswitch.co.za."
"Not interested" Thank them and end politely. Never push.
