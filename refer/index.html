How it works

Three steps. One is yours.

Questions

The small print.

Do I have to sell anything?

No. Just send the name. We contact the business and do all the selling ourselves.

When do I get the R500?

Once the business buys a website and their payment clears, we pay you by EFT within 7 days. If they don't buy, nothing is owed either way.

Is there a limit?

No. R500 for every business you send that buys, however many that is.

What if someone else sends the same business?

The first person to send it gets the R500. We'll tell you if a business is already on our list.

Do I need to tell the business?

It helps, but you don't have to. Only share details that are public or that the owner is happy for you to share. We always ask before we message anyone again.

Will you tell me what happened?

Yes. We'll let you know when we've reached out and if they buy.

Do I pay tax on it?

Referral rewards are income, so declare them if you need to.
