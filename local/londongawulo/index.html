Our story

Soweto flavour,
made by hand

London Gawulo was started by Chef Gawulo, Sethembile Mchunu: a passionate, self-taught chef from Orlando East. The bread is baked at home, the plates are grilled to order, and every box leaves the kitchen the way he would serve it to family.
