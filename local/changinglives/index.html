We show up where it counts.

Changing Lives Foundation is a registered non-profit based in Glen Austin, Midrand. We work in youth development, education and support for unemployed graduates, and we answer when communities are hit by disaster.

What we do

Relief

Disaster response

We are a disaster-response NGO of African origin. When a community is hit, we mobilise partners, supplies and volunteers to get help on the ground.
