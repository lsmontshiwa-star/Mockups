Good questions

Before you switch.

Who owns the web address?

If you already own your domain, it stays yours and we just connect it. If we buy one for you, we manage it while the service runs.

Do you need my passwords?

No. We never need your email, social media or other account logins.

What do you publish about my business?

Only what you supply or approve. We don't invent reviews, licences or claims.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, with no notice period. The service runs to the end of the month you paid for.

What if a payment fails?

You get a notice and 7 days to sort it out. The site stays up during that time.

How do I pay?

A secure card or EFT link for the setup, then a monthly debit order. No cash. Full details are in our client terms.
