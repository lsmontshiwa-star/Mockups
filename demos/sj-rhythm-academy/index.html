About Sipho

One of South Africa's elite drummers and educators.

Sipho SJ Malinga has been a professional drummer since 2000. He formalised his training at the University of Cape Town in Jazz Performance, holds a Trinity College London Grade 8 with Distinction and a Licentiate Diploma in Drum Performance, which few drumming educators in Africa hold.

He is the first Black DW Drums endorsee in South Africa, a Zildjian endorsed artist, the founder of Mpumalanga Arts Jams and SJ CreaTVs, and a former board member and instructor at Witbank Arts Campus.
