Why she started

At university in 2022, she noticed that the businesses she walked past every day barely showed up online. They were real and skilled, but invisible, and that capped how far they could grow.

She saw the same ceiling at Mjojo Finance Play, a theatre-based financial literacy company, where she built the brand and partnership systems from scratch. Growth there still depended on someone visiting schools in person.

Later, unemployed and freelancing, she kept solving the same problems by hand, so she decided to build systems that would solve them permanently.

Runs unattended Checks its own work Recovers when a tool fails Built with free tools

With no funding and no team, she taught herself to connect free AI tools into one automated pipeline. It finds businesses, verifies them with a second independent search, and writes personalised copy using Google's Gemini AI. It then builds and publishes a custom preview website for each business and prepares the outreach. Every business sees its own site before paying. The system runs unattended, checks its own work, and recovers when a tool fails.
