Welcome to Digital Switch

This form gets you set up: your details, your work number, how you'll be paid, and your signed documents. It takes about 10 minutes.

Have these ready

  • Proof of bank account: a bank confirmation letter, or the top of a statement showing your name and account (PDF or photo). If you'll be paid by Payoneer, you don't need this.
  • Your signed Contractor Agreement (PDF or a clear photo of each page).
  • Your signed Code of Conduct.
  • Your work number with WhatsApp set up, and the Digital Switch logo as its profile picture.

Your privacy

Your documents go to a private folder only Lethabo can open. We use your details only to work with you and pay you (POPIA). Please don't type bank account or ID numbers into the form: the document is enough.

Your warm list

Send each business you know through the referral form and tick "I'm a Digital Switch rep". That puts it on your desk and credits it to you.
