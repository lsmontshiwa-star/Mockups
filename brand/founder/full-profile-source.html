Lethabo Montshiwe is a 25-year-old Black South African entrepreneur and the founder of Digital Switch, a Johannesburg-based technology venture developing AI-powered systems that help small businesses and organisations become more visible, competitive and efficient.

The idea for Digital Switch began in 2022, when Lethabo noticed that many businesses and service providers she encountered in everyday life were almost invisible online. She recognised that the problem extended beyond digital visibility: businesses relying on manual processes, disconnected tools and limited capacity were often unable to grow beyond the constraints of their existing operations.

Determined to find better ways of solving these problems, Lethabo taught herself to work with AI tools, APIs and automation platforms, building Digital Switch's initial architecture without external funding. What began as manually identifying businesses on Google Maps evolved into a multi-stage automated system capable of sourcing businesses, researching and verifying information, qualifying prospects, discovering contacts, generating personalised content and building custom website previews. The architecture incorporates scheduled processing, checkpointing, automated quality checks and error recovery, allowing connected processes to run at scale.

The system has sourced 1,859 businesses and generated more than 170 personalised website previews. Around it, Lethabo has built the infrastructure to sell and deliver at scale: the Digital Switch website, where any business can request a free preview of its own site; sales desks that work on phone and laptop and give each sales representative their own leads; automatically generated offers for interested businesses; a referral programme; and automated client onboarding, hosting and support. Live sites include Kasi Lobby, a chain of gaming hubs in Alexandra, and Mjojo Finance Play. Digital Switch is now growing its team, with a local sales representative on board and sales roles open to young South Africans.

Her broader vision is to develop Digital Switch into an adaptable AI orchestration platform that identifies the capabilities required to solve a particular problem, discovers the best specialised tools available and connects them into complete, functional business systems.

Beyond technology, Lethabo founded Testimony Tuesday, creating a platform for people to share personal testimonies, explore scripture-based themes and encourage one another through bi-weekly online gatherings. She is also developing its reach through digital content and podcasting.

Her work is driven by the belief that African businesses and organisations should not be limited by a lack of access to the digital capabilities available in the modern economy. She is committed to building practical systems that help them grow, while creating opportunities for more young Africans to participate in the technology-driven economy.
